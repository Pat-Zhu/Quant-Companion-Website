Interview preparation

A process, not a pile of questions

The guide starts with how interviews work, then connects each stage to a preparation method. The selected questions are grouped by skill area so students can diagnose gaps and practice deliberately.

Read the complete guide →
  1. 1
    Map the process

    Understand screening, assessments, technical rounds, case work, and final interviews.

  2. 2
    Prioritize by role

    Use institution type and role expectations to decide what deserves the most practice.

  3. 3
    Practice in passes

    Solve unaided, review the failure mode, then answer a variation under time pressure.

  4. 4
    Review evidence

    Track accuracy, clarity, speed, and recurring mistakes—not just the number of questions completed.
